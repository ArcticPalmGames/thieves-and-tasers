Thieves and Tasers

Two sides. One heist.

Play quick PvP heists with friends. Thieves win by getting enough loot to the extraction point before time runs out. Guards win by stopping them.

Thieves

Climb a wall or smash a window to get inside. Slip past the Guards and haul the loot out. If a teammate gets caught, pay their bail to get them back in the round.

Guards

Lay mines and set up laser detectors to catch Thieves on the move. Use your jetpack to cut them off, then tase and cuff them.

Be there on day one.
